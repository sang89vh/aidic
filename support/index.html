Common questions

How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings → Plan and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription? On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I back up my words? Settings → Data management → Export creates a ZIP backup you can restore on any device.

Delete your Aidic account

  1. Open Aidic and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm. If you use Sign in with Apple, confirm with Apple when asked so the app can revoke Apple's sign-in tokens for Aidic.

Deletion is immediate. What is deleted and what is kept:

  • Deleted immediately: your Aidic sign-in account, and the plan, entitlement, AI-usage counts, device records, AI key lease and rate-limit records linked to it on our server.
  • Kept, with the link to your account removed: records of verified store purchases, only as needed for refunds, fraud prevention and tax obligations.
  • Not affected: words, reviews and progress saved on your device. Uninstall the app to remove them.

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it as described above.

If you no longer have the app, install it again, sign in with the same account and delete it from Settings. Questions about your data? Email sang89vh@icloud.com.
