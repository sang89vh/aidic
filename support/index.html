Common questions

How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings → Plan and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription? On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I back up my words? Settings → Data management → Export creates a ZIP backup you can restore on any device.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. Words saved on your device are kept. You can also email us to request deletion.
