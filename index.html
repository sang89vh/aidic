Aidic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Aidic ("the app") is a vocabulary-learning app made by SSG Team. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the app.

Learning data stays on your device

Your saved words, review history, progress, goals, achievements, generated stories, readings and podcasts, and downloaded audio are stored locally on your device. Aidic does not sync them to any server. You can export a ZIP backup or restore one from Settings → Data management, and clear cached audio and images there. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

AI requests

When you use an AI feature (word explanations, quizzes, stories, readings, podcasts, conversation practice, text-to-speech or pronunciation feedback), the content of that request is sent to the AI provider that answers it:

  • Aidic AI (the AI included with an Aidic account) uses OpenAI only.
  • Your own API key ("bring your own key") can be for OpenAI, Anthropic or Google (Gemini); requests go only to that key's provider. The key is stored in the device's secure storage (Keychain / Android Keystore).

Requests contain the text needed for the task: the words and text you look up or type, chat messages and text you import. Only when you use pronunciation practice, a short recording of your voice is sent to OpenAI to transcribe it. Requests do not include your name or contact details. Aidic AI requests carry a one-way hashed account identifier that helps OpenAI detect abuse.

Your permission comes first. Before the first request to any of these AI providers, the app asks whether you allow it (Allow / Don't allow). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → "Share requests with AI providers". While sharing is off, cloud AI features are turned off and nothing is sent to these providers.

Apple Intelligence

On supported Apple devices, short AI tasks can run on Apple's on-device model or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. These requests are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy terms; they are not sent to Aidic's servers or to the AI providers above, and they do not need the in-app AI-sharing permission.

Optional account

Signing in (with Google or Apple) is optional. It is needed for Aidic AI and for purchases. When you sign in, Google Firebase Authentication and Aidic's Firebase backend process:

  • an account identifier and the email address supplied by the sign-in provider,
  • your plan and purchase status (purchases are verified with Google Play or the App Store; Aidic never sees your payment details),
  • AI usage counts and short-term rate-limit records, used to enforce your plan's allowance and prevent abuse,
  • the AI key currently lent to your account for Aidic AI,
  • a random identifier created by the app on each device, with the platform and app version, used to limit how many devices share one account (not to track you).

Firebase App Check is used to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app.

Microphone

The microphone is used only in pronunciation practice: recording starts when you tap the record button and stops when you tap it again. The recording is sent to OpenAI only to transcribe it; the score is worked out on your device. Aidic does not store the recording on any server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, review reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to a server.

Who receives data and how it is protected

Aidic shares data only with these service providers, and only for the purposes described above:

  • OpenAI: AI requests for Aidic AI and for your own OpenAI key, including pronunciation recordings for transcription.
  • Anthropic and Google (Gemini): AI requests, only with your own key for that provider.
  • Google Firebase: sign-in, purchase verification, usage limits and App Check.
  • Apple: App Store purchases and, on supported devices, Apple Intelligence. Google Play verifies purchases on Android.

We only share data with providers that are bound by terms providing the same or equal protection of your data as stated in this policy and as required by Apple's App Store Review Guidelines. Each provider processes the data under its own privacy policy and API data terms.

What Aidic does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with data brokers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Your choices and withdrawing consent

  • AI sharing: turn off Settings → Privacy → "Share requests with AI providers" at any time.
  • Notifications: turn off the daily reminder in Settings, or turn off notifications for Aidic in your device's system settings.
  • Microphone: turn off microphone access for Aidic in your device's system settings (on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone).
  • Account: sign out at any time, or delete your account as described below.

Data retention

  • Learning data on your device: kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • AI request content and voice recordings: not stored on Aidic's servers. A recording is kept only as a temporary file on your device. AI providers keep requests only as long as their API data terms allow.
  • Account data (sign-in account, plan and entitlement, usage counts, rate-limit records, device records, AI key lease): kept while your account exists, and deleted when you delete the account.
  • Records of verified store purchases: kept only as long as needed for refunds, fraud prevention and tax obligations. When you delete your account, their link to it is removed and replaced by a one-way hash.
  • Server logs: kept for 30 days for security and troubleshooting.
  • If an account was blocked for abuse, that decision is kept as an operator record after the account is deleted.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Account → Delete account in the app and confirm. Deletion is immediate:

  • For Sign in with Apple accounts, the app first revokes Apple's sign-in tokens for Aidic.
  • Aidic's server then deletes your plan and entitlement, AI usage counts, device records, AI key lease and rate-limit records, and deletes your sign-in account.
  • Records of verified store purchases are kept as described above, with the link to your account removed.

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play settings. Words saved on your device are not affected; uninstall the app to remove them. Full steps: https://sang89vh.github.io/aidic/support/#delete-account

Children

Aidic is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Security

Data in transit to AI providers and Firebase is encrypted with HTTPS. API keys are kept in the platform's secure storage.
